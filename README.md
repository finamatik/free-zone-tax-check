# Free Zone 0% Corporate Tax Check

A single page, no build step, nothing sent anywhere. Enter total revenue, the share from mainland UAE customers and the share from customers outside the UAE, tick whether the activity is on the Qualifying Activities list, and the page shows the de minimis allowance, the non qualifying revenue, the headroom left and the five tax periods affected if the line is crossed.

Live version, with the write up and the FAQ: **https://finamatik.com/free-zone-tax-check**

## What it applies

- A Qualifying Free Zone Person may earn a small amount of revenue that does not qualify for the 0% rate. The allowance is the lower of 5% of total revenue or AED 5 million (Cabinet Decision 100 of 2023).
- Qualifying and Excluded Activities: Ministerial Decision 229 of 2025, which replaced Ministerial Decision 265 of 2023 with retroactive effect from 1 June 2023. Consultancy, accounting, marketing, software sold to customers, recruitment and most professional services are not on the list.
- Crossing the allowance loses the status for the tax period and the four following (Federal Decree-Law 47 of 2022, Article 18).
- Small Business Relief, extended to 31 December 2029 by Ministerial Decision 131 of 2026, is flagged where it may suit better.

Reviewed against the sources on 7 September 2026. General guidance on the figures entered, not advice on anyone's facts.

## Run it

Open `index.html` in a browser, or serve the folder with any static host. The page loads the Fraunces, Inter Tight and JetBrains Mono fonts from Google Fonts and nothing else from outside the file. The analytics tag on the live site is not in this copy.
